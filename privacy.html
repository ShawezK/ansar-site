Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Ansar is a personal scheduling assistant used only by its owner. It has no other users.

What Ansar accesses

  • Calendar events (calendar.events): to create and update events on Ansar's own calendars, and on the owner's calendar where the owner has shared it with Ansar.
  • Free/busy times (calendar.freebusy): to see when the owner is busy, without reading the details of those events.

Other assistants

Ansar works with a few advisory assistants that the owner runs on the same computer. They may suggest events or tasks. Only Ansar acts on a suggestion, within limits the owner has set. Only Ansar connects to Google, and the others never receive Google sign-in tokens or access.

When Ansar asks one of them a question, it removes sensitive personal details first, and includes only the calendar details needed for that question.

How the data is used and kept

  • Data is used only to plan and schedule the owner's tasks and meetings.
  • It is stored on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • To plan a schedule, the details needed for that task may be processed by an AI model provider acting for the owner. They are not used for any other purpose.

Ansar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
