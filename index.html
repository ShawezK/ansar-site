Ansar

Ansar is a personal scheduling assistant, built and used by one person for their own calendar. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Ansar is part of a small set of private assistants that the same person runs on their own computer. The others give advice only and may suggest calendar events. Ansar is the only one that acts, and the only one that connects to Google Calendar.

With the account owner's permission, Ansar creates and updates events on its own Google calendars, and on the owner's calendar where the owner has shared it with Ansar. It reads free/busy times, so that tasks are scheduled into open slots.
